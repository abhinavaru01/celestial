# Mathematical Modeling Capstone

> [!intro] A real modelling project: turn a messy real-world situation into mathematics, solve, and write it up.

## Modelling cycle

Modelling loops: simplify reality with assumptions, build equations, solve, then check against reality and refine. The write-up must state assumptions honestly.

## Judgement over technique

The hard part is deciding what to include and ignore. A simple model you understand beats a complex one you cannot justify — and this links directly to English argumentation and CS implementation.

```formula Key formulas
State assumptions explicitly
Simplest model that fits wins (parsimony)
Validate outputs against known real values
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Model a population doubling every 3 years, starting at 1000.
> >
> > **Solution.** N = 1000 · 2^(t/3). At t = 6 years: 1000 · 2² = 4000. State the assumption of constant doubling time.

## What you should be able to do

- Translate a real situation into a model
- Solve and interpret the model
- Communicate assumptions and limitations
