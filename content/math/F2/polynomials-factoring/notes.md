# Polynomials & Factoring Basics

> Adding, multiplying and factoring polynomials, including common factors and simple quadratics.

## Polynomials are number-like

Polynomials add, subtract and multiply just like numbers, keeping like terms together. Multiplying uses the distributive law repeatedly (FOIL for two binomials).

## Factoring reverses expansion

Factoring rewrites a sum as a product — pulling out common factors or splitting a quadratic into two binomials. It is the key to solving equations later.

## What you should be able to do

- Add, subtract and multiply polynomials
- Factor out common factors
- Factor simple quadratics
