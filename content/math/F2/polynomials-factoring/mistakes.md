# Common Mistakes — Polynomials & Factoring Basics

> Every learner hits these — spot them before they cost you.

- Forgetting the middle term when squaring a binomial: (a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b².

- Missing a common factor before trying to factor a quadratic.

- Sign errors when factoring x² − 5x + 6 into (x−2)(x−3).
