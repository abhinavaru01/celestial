# Tricks & Problem-Solving Techniques — Geometry II: Similarity, Pythagoras & Mensuration

> Faster, cleaner ways to handle this topic.

- For similar shapes, set up length₁/length₂ = scale factor and solve by cross-multiplying.

- Area scales by the square of the length ratio; volume by the cube.

- Recognise common Pythagorean triples (3-4-5, 5-12-13) to save time.
