# Probability & Statistics I

> [!intro] Probability as a proportion of outcomes, and measures of centre and spread.

## Probability is a proportion

The probability of an event is (favourable outcomes)/(total equally-likely outcomes) — a number between 0 and 1. It is ratio thinking applied to chance.

## Centre and spread

Mean, median and mode summarise where data sits; range and later measures describe how spread out it is. Choosing the right summary depends on the data's shape.

```formula Key formulas
P(event) = favourable outcomes ÷ total outcomes
P(not A) = 1 − P(A)
0 ≤ P ≤ 1
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A bag has 3 red and 5 blue balls. P(red)?
> >
> > **Solution.** P(red) = 3/(3+5) = 3/8 = 0.375. P(not red) = 1 − 3/8 = 5/8.

## What you should be able to do

- Compute simple probabilities
- Find mean, median and mode
- Describe spread with range
