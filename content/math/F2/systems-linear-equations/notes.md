# Systems of Linear Equations

> Solving two equations in two unknowns by substitution and elimination, and modelling with them.

## Two facts, two unknowns

A system pins down two unknowns using two independent equations. The solution is the single point where both lines cross.

## Substitution vs elimination

Substitution solves one equation for a variable and plugs it into the other; elimination adds or subtracts equations to cancel a variable. Pick whichever keeps the arithmetic simplest.

## What you should be able to do

- Solve systems by substitution and elimination
- Interpret a solution as the intersection of two lines
- Model two-condition problems with a system
