# Coordinate Geometry

> [!intro] Distance, midpoint and section formulas, and the equations of lines and circles.

## Geometry meets algebra

Placing shapes on the coordinate plane turns geometric questions into algebra. Distance between points is Pythagoras: √((x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²).

## Lines and circles

A line is y = mx + c or ax + by + c = 0; a circle centred at (h,k) with radius r is (x−h)² + (y−k)² = r². These equations let you compute intersections and tangents.

```formula Key formulas
Distance = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²)
Midpoint = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)
Circle: (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²
Perpendicular slopes multiply to −1
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Distance between (1, 2) and (4, 6)?
> >
> > **Solution.** √((4−1)² + (6−2)²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5.

## What you should be able to do

- Find equations of lines in various forms
- Recognise the equation of a circle
