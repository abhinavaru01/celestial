# Sequences & Series

> Arithmetic and geometric progressions, their sums, and links to growth and finance.

## AP and GP

An arithmetic progression adds a fixed difference each step; a geometric progression multiplies by a fixed ratio. Their nth-term formulas let you jump to any term without listing all of them.

## Sums and growth

Series sums have neat closed forms. A GP models compound growth (interest, populations) — the same "multiply by a factor" idea as successive percentages.

## What you should be able to do

- Identify AP and GP and find nth terms
- Sum arithmetic and geometric series
- Model compound growth
