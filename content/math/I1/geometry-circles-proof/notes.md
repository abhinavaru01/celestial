# Geometry III: Circles & Formal Proof

> Circle theorems and deductive proof as a skill in itself — reasoning that transfers to CS correctness.

## Circle theorems

Angles in a circle follow strict rules — the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference on the same arc; angles in a semicircle are right angles. These give powerful shortcuts.

## What a proof is

A proof is a chain of justified steps from given facts to a conclusion, each step backed by a reason. Learning to prove trains the same rigorous reasoning used to argue a program is correct.

## What you should be able to do

- Apply circle theorems (angles, tangents, chords)
- Construct a deductive proof
- Justify each step with a reason
