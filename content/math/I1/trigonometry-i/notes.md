# Trigonometry I

> Right-triangle ratios (sin, cos, tan) and early unit-circle intuition — the tools physics vectors need.

## SOH-CAH-TOA

In a right triangle, sine = opposite/hypotenuse, cosine = adjacent/hypotenuse, tangent = opposite/adjacent. These ratios connect angles to side lengths.

## The unit circle

On a circle of radius 1, a point at angle θ has coordinates (cos θ, sin θ). This extends trig beyond right triangles and is the foundation for waves and vectors.

## What you should be able to do

- Use sin, cos and tan in right triangles
- Solve for unknown sides and angles
- Understand the unit-circle picture
