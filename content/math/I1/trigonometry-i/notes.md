# Trigonometry I

> [!intro] Right-triangle ratios (sin, cos, tan) and early unit-circle intuition — the tools physics vectors need.

## SOH-CAH-TOA

In a right triangle, sine = opposite/hypotenuse, cosine = adjacent/hypotenuse, tangent = opposite/adjacent. These ratios connect angles to side lengths.

## The unit circle

On a circle of radius 1, a point at angle θ has coordinates (cos θ, sin θ). This extends trig beyond right triangles and is the foundation for waves and vectors.

```formula Key formulas
sin θ = opp/hyp, cos θ = adj/hyp, tan θ = opp/adj
On the unit circle: (cos θ, sin θ)
sin 30°=½, cos 60°=½, tan 45°=1
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A right triangle has hypotenuse 10 and angle 30°. Find the opposite side.
> >
> > **Solution.** sin 30° = opp/hyp → opp = 10 × sin 30° = 10 × 0.5 = 5.

## What you should be able to do

- Use sin, cos and tan in right triangles
- Solve for unknown sides and angles
- Understand the unit-circle picture
