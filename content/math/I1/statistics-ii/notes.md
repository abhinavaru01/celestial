# Statistics II

> [!intro] Grouped data, measures of dispersion, and reading real studies critically.

## Grouped data

When data comes in intervals, we estimate the mean using midpoints weighted by frequency. This scales statistics to large real datasets.

## Dispersion

Two datasets can share a mean yet differ wildly in spread. Variance and standard deviation quantify that spread — essential for interpreting biology and physics measurements.

```formula Key formulas
Grouped mean ≈ Σ(midpoint × frequency) ÷ Σfrequency
Variance = mean of squared deviations
Standard deviation = √variance
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Data 2, 4, 4, 6 has mean 4. Find its standard deviation.
> >
> > **Solution.** Deviations: −2,0,0,2 → squares 4,0,0,4 → variance = 8/4 = 2. SD = √2 ≈ 1.41.
