# Common Mistakes — Algebra as Generalized Arithmetic

> Every learner hits these — spot them before they cost you.

- Combining unlike terms, e.g. writing 3x + 2 = 5x.

- Forgetting to distribute to every term: 2(x + 3) = 2x + 6, not 2x + 3.

- Dropping the sign when moving a term across an equals sign.
