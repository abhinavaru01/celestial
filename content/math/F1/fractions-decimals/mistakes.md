# Common Mistakes — Fractions & Decimals as One System

> Every learner hits these — spot them before they cost you.

- Adding fractions by adding numerators and denominators (1/2 + 1/3 ≠ 2/5).

- Forgetting to find a common denominator before adding or comparing.

- Confusing "divide by a fraction" with "divide the fraction" — you multiply by the reciprocal.
