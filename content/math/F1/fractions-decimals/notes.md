# Fractions & Decimals as One System

> Fractions, decimals and division are three views of the same idea — parts of a whole on the number line.

## A fraction is a division

The fraction a/b literally means a ÷ b. That is why 3/4 = 0.75 — dividing 3 by 4. Seeing fractions and decimals as the same number written two ways removes the sense that they are separate topics.

## Common denominators

You can only add or compare fractions once the pieces are the same size — a common denominator. Multiplying top and bottom by the same number renames a fraction without changing its value.

## Multiplying vs dividing

Multiplying fractions multiplies the parts (½ of ⅓ = ⅙). Dividing by a fraction multiplies by its reciprocal — "flip and multiply" — because dividing by ½ asks "how many halves fit?".

## What you should be able to do

- Convert freely between fractions and decimals
- Add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions
- Place fractions and decimals correctly on the number line
