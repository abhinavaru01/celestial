# Tricks & Problem-Solving Techniques — Fractions & Decimals as One System

> Faster, cleaner ways to handle this topic.

- To compare fractions quickly, cross-multiply: 3/4 vs 5/7 → 21 vs 20, so 3/4 is bigger.

- "Flip and multiply" turns any fraction division into a multiplication.

- Simplify before multiplying to keep numbers small (cancel common factors first).
