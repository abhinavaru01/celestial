# Data & Graphs I

> [!intro] Reading and drawing bar charts, pictographs and line graphs — and spotting misleading ones.

## Graphs turn numbers into pictures

A well-chosen graph makes patterns visible at a glance. Bar charts compare categories; line graphs show change over time; pictographs scale a symbol to a quantity.

## Misleading graphs

A truncated axis or an inconsistent scale can exaggerate or hide a trend. Reading the axes first is the habit that protects you from being fooled — and this data-literacy feeds every science subject.

```formula Key formulas
Mean = (sum of values) ÷ (number of values)
Median = middle value of ordered data
Mode = most frequent value
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Find the mean and median of 3, 7, 7, 2, 6.
> >
> > **Solution.** Mean = (3+7+7+2+6)/5 = 25/5 = 5. Ordered: 2,3,6,7,7 → median = 6.

## What you should be able to do

- Read and construct bar charts, pictographs and line graphs
- Compute simple averages from data
- Recognise misleading graphs
