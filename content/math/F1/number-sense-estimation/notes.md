# Number Sense & Estimation

> Place value, negative numbers, orders of magnitude, mental math and the discipline of estimating before computing.

## The number line as one model

Every number — positive, negative, whole or decimal — lives at one point on the number line. Negatives are mirror images of positives across zero; adding moves right, subtracting moves left. Holding this single picture prevents most sign confusion.

## Estimation before calculation

Rounding each number to one significant figure gives a quick "ballpark" you can compare your exact answer against. If the exact answer is far from the estimate, you slipped a decimal or a sign — estimation is your error detector.

## Orders of magnitude

Thinking in powers of ten (is this in the tens, thousands, millions?) lets you reason about very large or small quantities without getting lost in digits.

## What you should be able to do

- Work fluently with negative numbers and place value
- Estimate answers using rounding and orders of magnitude
- Use mental-math strategies to compute quickly
