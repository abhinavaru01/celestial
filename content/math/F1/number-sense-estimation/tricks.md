# Tricks & Problem-Solving Techniques — Number Sense & Estimation

> Faster, cleaner ways to handle this topic.

- Round to one significant figure to sanity-check every answer.

- Break awkward sums into friendly parts: 47 + 38 = (47 + 3) + 35 = 85.

- To subtract, "add up" from the smaller to the larger number instead of borrowing.
