# Linear Equations in One Variable

> Solving equations by keeping both sides balanced, and using them to model word problems directly.

## The balance model

An equation is a balance: whatever you do to one side you must do to the other. Undo operations in reverse order (inverse of +, ×, etc.) to isolate the variable.

## From words to equations

Assign a letter to the unknown, translate each phrase into algebra, then solve. This replaces years of arithmetic-only guessing with one reliable method.

## What you should be able to do

- Solve linear equations by inverse operations
- Keep an equation balanced at every step
- Model word problems with a single equation
