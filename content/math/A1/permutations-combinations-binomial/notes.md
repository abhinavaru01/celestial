# Permutations, Combinations & Binomial Theorem

> Advanced counting and the binomial expansion of (a + b)ⁿ.

## Binomial theorem

(a + b)ⁿ expands with coefficients nCr — the same combinations from counting. Pascal's triangle lists these coefficients row by row.

## Choosing terms

The general term nCr·a^(n−r)·b^r lets you pick any single term (e.g., the x³ coefficient) without expanding everything.

## What you should be able to do

- Apply advanced counting arguments
- Expand binomials with the binomial theorem
- Find specific terms in an expansion
