# Permutations, Combinations & Binomial Theorem

> [!intro] Advanced counting and the binomial expansion of (a + b)ⁿ.

## Binomial theorem

(a + b)ⁿ expands with coefficients nCr — the same combinations from counting. Pascal's triangle lists these coefficients row by row.

## Choosing terms

The general term nCr·a^(n−r)·b^r lets you pick any single term (e.g., the x³ coefficient) without expanding everything.

```formula Key formulas
(a + b)ⁿ = Σ nCr · a^(n−r) · b^r
General term: T_(r+1) = nCr · a^(n−r) · b^r
Coefficients from Pascal’s triangle
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Find the coefficient of x² in (1 + x)⁵.
> >
> > **Solution.** Term is 5C2 · x² = 10x². Coefficient = 10.
