# Integration Techniques & Applications

> [!intro] Substitution and by-parts integration, definite integrals, and areas and volumes.

## Techniques

Substitution reverses the chain rule; integration by parts reverses the product rule. Recognising which to use is pattern recognition built by practice.

## Definite integrals and applications

A definite integral gives an exact accumulated total between limits — area under a curve, volume of revolution, total displacement. It turns "adding infinitely many pieces" into one number.

```formula Key formulas
Substitution: let u = inner function whose derivative appears
By parts: ∫u dv = uv − ∫v du (choose u by LIATE)
Definite integral gives net area between limits
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Evaluate ∫ 2x·(x² + 1)³ dx.
> >
> > **Solution.** Let u = x² + 1, du = 2x dx → ∫ u³ du = u⁴/4 + C = (x² + 1)⁴/4 + C.

## What you should be able to do

- Integrate by substitution and by parts
- Evaluate definite integrals
- Find areas and volumes of revolution
