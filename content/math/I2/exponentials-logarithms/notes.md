# Exponentials & Logarithms

> [!intro] Exponential growth and decay, logarithms as the inverse, and their laws — used across chemistry, biology and CS.

## Exponential change

Exponential functions grow or decay by a constant factor per step — populations, radioactive decay, compound interest. The rate of change is proportional to the current amount.

## Logarithms invert exponentials

log_b(x) answers "what power of b gives x?". Log laws (log(ab)=log a+log b) turn multiplication into addition — the reason logs simplify pH, decibels and algorithmic complexity.

```formula Key formulas
log_b(xy) = log_b x + log_b y
log_b(xⁿ) = n log_b x
b^x = y ⇔ log_b y = x
Growth: N = N₀ · e^(kt)
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Solve 2^x = 32.
> >
> > **Solution.** Take logs: x = log₂ 32 = 5, since 2⁵ = 32.
