# Calculus Intuition: Derivatives

> Rate of change made exact — the derivative as the slope of a curve, taught alongside kinematics.

## Instantaneous rate

The derivative measures how fast a quantity changes at an instant — the slope of the tangent to its graph. It makes the "slope = rate" idea from lines exact for curves.

## Power rule

For f(x) = xⁿ, the derivative is n·x^(n−1). This single rule differentiates polynomials and connects directly to velocity being the derivative of position.

## What you should be able to do

- Interpret the derivative as an instantaneous rate
- Differentiate simple powers
- Connect derivative to slope of a tangent
