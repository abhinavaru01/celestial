# Calculus Intuition: Integrals

> [!intro] Accumulation and area under a curve — the reverse of differentiation.

## Area as accumulation

The integral adds up infinitely many thin slices to give the area under a curve — which represents total accumulated quantity (e.g., distance from a velocity graph).

## The reverse of the derivative

Integration undoes differentiation: ∫xⁿ dx = x^(n+1)/(n+1) + C. This Fundamental Theorem links the two halves of calculus.

```formula Key formulas
∫ xⁿ dx = x^(n+1)/(n+1) + C  (n ≠ −1)
Definite integral = signed area under the curve
Integration undoes differentiation
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Evaluate ∫ from 0 to 3 of 2x dx.
> >
> > **Solution.** ∫ 2x dx = x². Evaluate 0→3: 3² − 0² = 9. (This is the area of the triangle under y = 2x.)

## What you should be able to do

- Interpret an integral as accumulated area
- Integrate simple powers
- Relate integration and differentiation
