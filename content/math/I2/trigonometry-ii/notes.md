# Trigonometry II

> [!intro] Identities, trigonometric equations, and the sine and cosine rules for any triangle.

## Identities

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 and the addition formulas let you rewrite and simplify trig expressions. Identities are the algebra of trigonometry.

## Rules for any triangle

The sine rule (a/sin A = b/sin B) and cosine rule (c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C) extend trig to non-right triangles — vital in physics and surveying.

```formula Key formulas
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
Sine rule: a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C
Cosine rule: c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A triangle has sides a=5, b=7 with included angle C=60°. Find c.
> >
> > **Solution.** c² = 5² + 7² − 2·5·7·cos60° = 25 + 49 − 70(0.5) = 74 − 35 = 39 → c = √39 ≈ 6.24.

- Apply the sine and cosine rules
