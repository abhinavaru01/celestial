# Thermal Physics

> The gas laws and kinetic theory — a topic shared with chemistry, taught once.

## Gas laws

Pressure, volume and temperature of a gas are linked: PV/T is constant, giving the ideal gas equation PV = nRT. Each individual law (Boyle, Charles) is a special case.

## Kinetic theory

A gas is countless particles in random motion; temperature measures their average kinetic energy. This microscopic picture explains the macroscopic gas laws.

## What you should be able to do

- Apply the gas laws
- Relate temperature to particle motion
- Use the ideal gas equation
