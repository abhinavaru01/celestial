# Electricity II

> EMF and internal resistance, Kirchhoff's laws, and capacitors (introductory).

## Real sources

A real cell has internal resistance, so its terminal voltage drops under load. EMF is the total energy per charge; some is lost inside the source.

## Kirchhoff's laws

Charge conservation gives the junction (current) law; energy conservation gives the loop (voltage) law. Together they solve any circuit.

## What you should be able to do

- Use EMF and internal resistance
- Apply Kirchhoff's laws
- Explain capacitor basics
