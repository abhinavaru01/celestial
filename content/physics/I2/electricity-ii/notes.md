# Electricity II

> [!intro] EMF and internal resistance, Kirchhoff's laws, and capacitors (introductory).

## Real sources

A real cell has internal resistance, so its terminal voltage drops under load. EMF is the total energy per charge; some is lost inside the source.

## Kirchhoff's laws

Charge conservation gives the junction (current) law; energy conservation gives the loop (voltage) law. Together they solve any circuit.

```formula Key formulas
EMF ε = I(R + r), r = internal resistance
Kirchhoff current law: Σ currents in = Σ out
Kirchhoff voltage law: Σ voltages around a loop = 0
Capacitor: Q = CV
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A cell of EMF 6 V and internal resistance 0.5 Ω drives 2 A. Find the terminal voltage.
> >
> > **Solution.** V = ε − Ir = 6 − 2(0.5) = 5 V.
