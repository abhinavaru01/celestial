# Oscillations (Simple Harmonic Motion)

> Simple harmonic motion: restoring force, period, and energy exchange in oscillators.

## Restoring force

SHM happens when the restoring force is proportional to displacement and directed back to equilibrium (F = −kx). Springs and small-angle pendulums are examples.

## Period and energy

The period depends on the system (mass and stiffness), not the amplitude. Energy sloshes between kinetic and potential twice per cycle.

## What you should be able to do

- Define SHM conditions
- Relate period to system parameters
- Describe energy in oscillation
