# Measurement, Units & Estimation

> SI units, unit conversion as ratio reasoning, significant figures and Fermi estimation.

## Measurement needs units

A number without a unit is meaningless in physics. SI units (metre, kilogram, second) form a consistent system, and converting between units is just scaling a ratio.

## Significant figures and estimation

Significant figures communicate precision honestly. Fermi estimation — breaking a hard quantity into rough factors — gives a defensible ballpark and checks detailed answers.

## What you should be able to do

- Use SI units and convert between them
- Apply significant figures
- Make order-of-magnitude estimates
