# Energy I

> [!intro] Work, kinetic and potential energy, and conservation of energy as an accounting principle.

## Work transfers energy

Work = force × distance in the direction of the force. Doing work on an object changes its energy — the work-energy link.

## Conservation as accounting

Energy is neither created nor destroyed, only transformed. Total energy before = total after; tracking KE (½mv²) and PE (mgh) like a ledger solves many problems without forces.

```formula Key formulas
Work W = F·d (force along motion)
Kinetic energy KE = ½mv²
Potential energy PE = mgh
Energy is conserved (transforms, not lost)
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A 2 kg ball is dropped from 5 m. Find its speed just before impact (g = 10).
> >
> > **Solution.** PE → KE: mgh = ½mv² → v = √(2gh) = √(2·10·5) = √100 = 10 m/s.

## What you should be able to do

- Calculate work, KE and PE
- Apply conservation of energy
- Relate work done to energy change
