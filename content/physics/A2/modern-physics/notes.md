# Modern Physics

> The photoelectric effect, atomic models, nuclei and radioactivity, and semiconductor basics.

## Quantum beginnings

The photoelectric effect shows light comes in packets (photons) with energy E = hf. This launched quantum physics and explained atomic spectra.

## Nuclei and radioactivity

Unstable nuclei decay (alpha, beta, gamma), each decay halving the sample over one half-life. Mass converts to energy via E = mc².

## What you should be able to do

- Explain the photoelectric effect
- Describe atomic and nuclear models
- Understand radioactivity and half-life
