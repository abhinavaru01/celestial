# Modern Physics

> [!intro] The photoelectric effect, atomic models, nuclei and radioactivity, and semiconductor basics.

## Quantum beginnings

The photoelectric effect shows light comes in packets (photons) with energy E = hf. This launched quantum physics and explained atomic spectra.

## Nuclei and radioactivity

Unstable nuclei decay (alpha, beta, gamma), each decay halving the sample over one half-life. Mass converts to energy via E = mc².

```formula Key formulas
Photon energy E = hf
Mass–energy E = mc²
After n half-lives, fraction remaining = (1/2)ⁿ
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A radioactive sample has a half-life of 5 years. What fraction remains after 15 years?
> >
> > **Solution.** 15/5 = 3 half-lives → (1/2)³ = 1/8 = 0.125 remains.
