# Magnetic Effects & Electromagnetic Induction

> [!intro] Magnetic forces, Faraday's and Lenz's laws, and AC circuits — using differential equations.

## Induction

A changing magnetic flux induces an EMF (Faraday); the induced current opposes the change (Lenz). This is how generators and transformers work.

## AC circuits

Alternating current varies sinusoidally; inductors and capacitors respond to its rate of change, described naturally by differential equations.

```formula Key formulas
Faraday: EMF = −dΦ/dt (Φ = magnetic flux)
Lenz: induced current opposes the change
Only changing flux induces an EMF
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Magnetic flux through a coil changes from 0.2 to 0.6 Wb in 0.1 s. Find the magnitude of the induced EMF.
> >
> > **Solution.** EMF = ΔΦ/Δt = (0.6 − 0.2)/0.1 = 0.4/0.1 = 4 V.

## What you should be able to do

- Apply Faraday's and Lenz's laws
- Analyse simple AC circuits
