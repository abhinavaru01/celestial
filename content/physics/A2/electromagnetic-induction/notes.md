# Magnetic Effects & Electromagnetic Induction

> Magnetic forces, Faraday's and Lenz's laws, and AC circuits — using differential equations.

## Induction

A changing magnetic flux induces an EMF (Faraday); the induced current opposes the change (Lenz). This is how generators and transformers work.

## AC circuits

Alternating current varies sinusoidally; inductors and capacitors respond to its rate of change, described naturally by differential equations.

## What you should be able to do

- Compute magnetic force on currents/charges
- Apply Faraday's and Lenz's laws
- Analyse simple AC circuits
