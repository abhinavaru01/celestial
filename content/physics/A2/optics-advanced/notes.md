# Optics at Full Rigor

> Wave optics: interference, diffraction and the wave nature of light.

## Wave optics

Light's wave nature shows up as interference (double-slit fringes) and diffraction (spreading around edges). Path difference of a whole wavelength gives bright fringes.

## Evidence for waves

These effects cannot be explained by rays alone — they were the historic proof that light is a wave, later refined by quantum theory.

## What you should be able to do

- Explain interference and diffraction
- Analyse the double-slit experiment
- Relate path difference to fringes
