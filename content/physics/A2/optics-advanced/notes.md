# Optics at Full Rigor

> [!intro] Wave optics: interference, diffraction and the wave nature of light.

## Wave optics

Light's wave nature shows up as interference (double-slit fringes) and diffraction (spreading around edges). Path difference of a whole wavelength gives bright fringes.

## Evidence for waves

These effects cannot be explained by rays alone — they were the historic proof that light is a wave, later refined by quantum theory.

```formula Key formulas
Bright fringe: path difference = nλ
Double slit fringe spacing y = λD/d
Narrow slit ⇒ more diffraction
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** In a double slit, slit spacing d = 0.5 mm, screen distance D = 2 m, λ = 500 nm. Find the fringe spacing.
> >
> > **Solution.** y = λD/d = (500×10⁻⁹ × 2)/(0.5×10⁻³) = (10⁻⁶)/(0.5×10⁻³) = 2×10⁻³ m = 2 mm.
