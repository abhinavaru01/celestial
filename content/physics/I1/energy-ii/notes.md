# Energy II

> [!intro] Power, efficiency and energy resources, treated quantitatively.

## Power and efficiency

Power is the rate of doing work (P = W/t). Efficiency is useful output ÷ total input — always below 100% because some energy dissipates as heat.

## Energy in the real world

Every device transforms energy with losses. Comparing resources means comparing energy density, renewability and conversion efficiency.

```formula Key formulas
Power P = W/t = energy/time
Efficiency = (useful output ÷ total input) × 100%
1 watt = 1 joule/second
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A motor does 600 J of useful work from 800 J input in 4 s. Find its power output and efficiency.
> >
> > **Solution.** Power (useful) = 600/4 = 150 W. Efficiency = 600/800 × 100 = 75%.

## What you should be able to do

- Calculate power and efficiency
- Track energy through transformations
- Compare energy resources
