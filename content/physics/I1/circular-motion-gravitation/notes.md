# Circular Motion & Gravitation

> Centripetal acceleration, orbits and Newton's law of gravitation.

## Centripetal force

Circular motion at constant speed still accelerates — toward the centre. A net centripetal force (mv²/r) is required; remove it and the object flies off tangentially.

## Gravitation

Every mass attracts every other with F = Gm₁m₂/r². This same force keeps planets in orbit, balancing the centripetal requirement.

## What you should be able to do

- Explain centripetal force
- Use the gravitation law
- Describe orbital motion
