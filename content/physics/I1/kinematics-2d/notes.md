# Kinematics in Two Dimensions

> Vectors applied to motion — resolving into components and analysing projectiles.

## Independence of components

Horizontal and vertical motion are independent: gravity affects only the vertical. Splitting a velocity into x and y components (using trig) turns 2-D motion into two 1-D problems.

## Projectiles

A projectile has constant horizontal velocity and vertical acceleration g. Combining the two components gives its parabolic path, range and time of flight.

## What you should be able to do

- Resolve velocity into components
- Analyse projectile motion
- Treat horizontal and vertical motion independently
