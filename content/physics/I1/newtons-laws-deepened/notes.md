# Newton's Laws Deepened

> [!intro] Friction, inclined planes and connected systems of bodies.

## Friction

Friction opposes relative motion and is proportional to the normal force (F ≤ μN). It converts kinetic energy to heat and must be added to force diagrams.

## Inclines and systems

On a slope, resolve weight into components along and perpendicular to the surface. For connected bodies, apply F = ma to each and to the system.

```formula Key formulas
Friction f = μN
On an incline: along-slope mg sin θ, into-surface mg cos θ
Connected bodies share one acceleration
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A 10 kg block on a 30° frictionless incline. Find its acceleration (g = 10).
> >
> > **Solution.** a = g sin θ = 10 × sin30° = 10 × 0.5 = 5 m/s² down the slope.

## What you should be able to do

- Include friction in force analysis
- Solve inclined-plane problems
- Analyse connected bodies
