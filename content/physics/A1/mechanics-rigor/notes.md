# Mechanics at Full Rigor

> [!intro] Dynamics with calculus, the work-energy theorem, and rotational motion of rigid bodies.

## Work-energy theorem

The net work done on a body equals its change in kinetic energy. With calculus, this handles varying forces that SUVAT cannot.

## Rotation

Rotating bodies have angular versions of every linear quantity: torque (force), moment of inertia (mass), angular momentum. Newton's laws reappear in rotational form.

```formula Key formulas
Work–energy theorem: W_net = ΔKE
Torque τ = r × F = rF sin θ
Angular momentum conserved with no external torque
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A 3 kg object speeds up from 2 to 5 m/s. Find the net work done.
> >
> > **Solution.** W = ΔKE = ½m(v² − u²) = ½·3·(25 − 4) = ½·3·21 = 31.5 J.
