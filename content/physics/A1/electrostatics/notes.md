# Electrostatics

> Electric charge, fields, potential and capacitance, using calculus and vectors.

## Charge and field

Like charges repel, unlike attract, with force F = kq₁q₂/r² (an inverse-square law like gravity). The electric field E = F/q describes force per unit charge.

## Potential and capacitance

Electric potential is energy per unit charge; the field points from high to low potential. Capacitance measures stored charge per volt.

## What you should be able to do

- Use Coulomb's law and electric fields
- Relate field and potential
- Analyse capacitance
