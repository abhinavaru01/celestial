# Electrostatics

> [!intro] Electric charge, fields, potential and capacitance, using calculus and vectors.

## Charge and field

Like charges repel, unlike attract, with force F = kq₁q₂/r² (an inverse-square law like gravity). The electric field E = F/q describes force per unit charge.

## Potential and capacitance

Electric potential is energy per unit charge; the field points from high to low potential. Capacitance measures stored charge per volt.

```formula Key formulas
Coulomb: F = kq₁q₂/r²
Field E = F/q
Field points high → low potential
Capacitance C = Q/V
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Two charges are separated, then the distance is halved. How does the force change?
> >
> > **Solution.** F ∝ 1/r². Halving r gives F × (1/(1/2)²) = ×4 the original force.
