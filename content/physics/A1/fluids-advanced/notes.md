# Fluids at A-Level

> Fluid pressure, viscosity and an introduction to Bernoulli's principle.

## Static and moving fluids

Pressure in a static fluid rises with depth (P = ρgh). In steady flow, the continuity equation says a narrower pipe means faster flow.

## Bernoulli

Where a fluid moves faster, its pressure drops. This explains lift, spray guns and curveballs — energy conservation for flowing fluids.

## What you should be able to do

- Analyse pressure in static and moving fluids
- Explain viscosity and flow
- Apply Bernoulli qualitatively
