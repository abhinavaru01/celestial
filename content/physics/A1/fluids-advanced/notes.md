# Fluids at A-Level

> [!intro] Fluid pressure, viscosity and an introduction to Bernoulli's principle.

## Static and moving fluids

Pressure in a static fluid rises with depth (P = ρgh). In steady flow, the continuity equation says a narrower pipe means faster flow.

## Bernoulli

Where a fluid moves faster, its pressure drops. This explains lift, spray guns and curveballs — energy conservation for flowing fluids.

```formula Key formulas
Static pressure P = ρgh
Continuity: A₁v₁ = A₂v₂
Bernoulli: faster flow ↔ lower pressure
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Water flows through a pipe that narrows from area 4 cm² to 1 cm². If inlet speed is 2 m/s, find the outlet speed.
> >
> > **Solution.** A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ → 4×2 = 1×v₂ → v₂ = 8 m/s.

## What you should be able to do

- Analyse pressure in static and moving fluids
- Explain viscosity and flow
- Apply Bernoulli qualitatively
