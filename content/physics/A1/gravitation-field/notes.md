# Gravitation (Field Treatment)

> [!intro] Gravitational fields, potential and energy, and orbital mechanics at depth.

## Fields and potential

A gravitational field g = F/m describes the force per unit mass at each point. Gravitational potential and PE track the energy of a mass in the field.

## Orbital energy

An orbit's total energy (kinetic + potential) determines its shape and whether it is bound. Escape velocity is the speed needed to reach zero total energy.

```formula Key formulas
Field g = GM/r²
Escape velocity v = √(2GM/r)
Bound orbit → negative total energy
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** At what multiple of Earth’s radius does g fall to 1/4 of its surface value?
> >
> > **Solution.** g ∝ 1/r². g/4 when r² = 4 → r = 2R. So at twice Earth’s radius from the centre.
