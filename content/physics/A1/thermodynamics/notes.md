# Thermodynamics

> The laws of thermodynamics, heat engines and entropy — sharing a spine with chemistry.

## First and second laws

The first law is energy conservation: ΔU = Q − W. The second says entropy (disorder) of an isolated system never decreases — setting a direction for time and limits on engines.

## Heat engines

Engines convert heat to work but never fully; the second law caps their efficiency. This links directly to chemistry's energetics.

## What you should be able to do

- State the laws of thermodynamics
- Analyse heat engines and efficiency
- Explain entropy intuitively
