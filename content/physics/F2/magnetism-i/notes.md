# Magnetism I

> [!intro] Magnetic fields, electromagnets and the link between electricity and magnetism.

## Fields and poles

Magnets have north and south poles; like poles repel, unlike attract. Field lines run from north to south and show the field's direction and strength.

## Electromagnetism

A current creates a magnetic field around it. Coiling wire into a solenoid concentrates the field, making an electromagnet — the basis of motors and relays.

```formula Key formulas
Like poles repel, unlike attract
Field lines run N → S outside a magnet
A current creates a magnetic field (right-hand grip rule)
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** How can you strengthen an electromagnet?
> >
> > **Solution.** Increase the current, add more coil turns, or insert a soft-iron core — each increases the magnetic field.
