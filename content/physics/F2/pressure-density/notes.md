# Pressure & Density

> [!intro] Density as mass per volume, pressure in fluids, and buoyancy — more ratio reasoning applied.

## Density and pressure are rates

Density = mass/volume and pressure = force/area — both are ratios. Pressure in a fluid increases with depth and acts equally in all directions.

## Buoyancy

A submerged object feels an upthrust equal to the weight of fluid it displaces (Archimedes). It floats when upthrust balances weight.

```formula Key formulas
Density ρ = m/V
Pressure P = F/A
Fluid pressure P = ρgh
Floats if ρ_object < ρ_fluid
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Find the pressure at the base of a 2 m column of water (ρ = 1000, g = 10).
> >
> > **Solution.** P = ρgh = 1000 × 10 × 2 = 20000 Pa = 20 kPa.
