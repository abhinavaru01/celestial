# Electricity I

> [!intro] Current, voltage, resistance and Ohm's law, with series and parallel circuits.

## Ohm's law

Voltage drives current through resistance: V = IR. It is the central relationship of simple circuits and, like many physics laws, a proportionality.

## Series vs parallel

In series, current is the same and resistances add; in parallel, voltage is the same and currents split. These rules follow from conservation of charge and energy.

```formula Key formulas
V = IR (Ohm’s law)
Series: R = R₁ + R₂ + …
Parallel: 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + …
Series current is uniform; parallel voltage is uniform
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Two 4 Ω resistors in series across 8 V. Find the current.
> >
> > **Solution.** R = 4 + 4 = 8 Ω. I = V/R = 8/8 = 1 A.
