# Population Biology

> Exponential and logistic growth, and what limits populations.

## Growth models

Unchecked populations grow exponentially; real ones level off (logistic) as resources limit them at the carrying capacity — reusing exponentials from maths.

## Limiting factors

Food, space, predators and disease limit growth. Understanding them predicts population dynamics and informs conservation.

## What you should be able to do

- Model population growth
- Explain limiting factors
- Use exponential/logistic ideas
