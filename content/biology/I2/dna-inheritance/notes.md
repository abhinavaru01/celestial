# DNA & the Molecular Basis of Inheritance

> [!intro] DNA structure, replication and the central dogma — information flow in cells.

## DNA stores information

DNA is a double helix; the base sequence encodes instructions. Complementary base pairing (A-T, C-G) lets it copy itself faithfully.

## Central dogma

Information flows DNA → RNA → protein: genes are transcribed to RNA and translated to proteins that do the work. This information model unifies molecular biology.

```formula Key facts
Base pairing: A–T, C–G
Central dogma: DNA → RNA → protein
Complementary pairing enables replication
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A DNA strand reads A-T-G-C. What is the complementary strand?
> >
> > **Solution.** Pair A–T and C–G: the complement is T-A-C-G.
