# Evolution & Natural Selection

> The unifying theory of biology: evidence and mechanism of natural selection.

## Natural selection

Individuals vary; those better suited to the environment survive and reproduce more, passing on their traits. Over generations, populations adapt.

## The unifying theory

Evolution explains the diversity and relatedness of life. Evidence comes from fossils, anatomy, and DNA — a single framework for all biology.

## What you should be able to do

- Explain natural selection
- Cite evidence for evolution
- Relate variation to adaptation
