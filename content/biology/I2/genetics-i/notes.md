# Genetics I: Inheritance

> [!intro] Mendel's laws and monohybrid/dihybrid crosses — genetics as probability.

## Genes come in pairs

Each trait has two alleles, one from each parent; dominant alleles mask recessive ones. Mendel discovered these inheritance patterns from breeding experiments.

## Genetics is probability

Punnett squares predict offspring ratios (like 3:1) — this is exactly the probability from maths applied to inheritance, an interleaving in action.

```formula Key facts
Dominant allele masks recessive
Genotype = alleles; phenotype = appearance
Aa × Aa → 3 : 1 phenotype ratio
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Two heterozygous tall plants (Tt) are crossed. What fraction of offspring are short (tt)?
> >
> > **Solution.** Punnett square Tt × Tt → TT, Tt, Tt, tt. Short (tt) = 1/4 of offspring.
