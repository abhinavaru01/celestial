# Genetics I: Inheritance

> Mendel's laws and monohybrid/dihybrid crosses — genetics as probability.

## Genes come in pairs

Each trait has two alleles, one from each parent; dominant alleles mask recessive ones. Mendel discovered these inheritance patterns from breeding experiments.

## Genetics is probability

Punnett squares predict offspring ratios (like 3:1) — this is exactly the probability from maths applied to inheritance, an interleaving in action.

## What you should be able to do

- Use Mendelian ratios
- Complete Punnett squares
- Explain dominant and recessive alleles
