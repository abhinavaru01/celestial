# Nutrition & the Human Digestive System

> [!intro] Nutrients, a balanced diet, and how the digestive system breaks down food for energy.

## Nutrients and energy

Carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins and minerals each have roles; food is the body's energy input — linking to the energy accounting seen in physics.

## Digestion

Digestion breaks large food molecules into small absorbable ones, mechanically and by enzymes, as food passes through the gut.

```formula Key facts
Enzymes carry out chemical digestion
Absorption occurs mainly in the small intestine
Balanced diet: carbs, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, fibre, water
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Where is most digested food absorbed, and what adaptation helps?
> >
> > **Solution.** The small intestine — its villi give a huge surface area with a rich blood supply for fast absorption.

## What you should be able to do

- Identify major nutrient groups
- Trace food through digestion
- Link nutrition to energy
