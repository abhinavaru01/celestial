# Nutrition & the Human Digestive System

> Nutrients, a balanced diet, and how the digestive system breaks down food for energy.

## Nutrients and energy

Carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins and minerals each have roles; food is the body's energy input — linking to the energy accounting seen in physics.

## Digestion

Digestion breaks large food molecules into small absorbable ones, mechanically and by enzymes, as food passes through the gut.

## What you should be able to do

- Identify major nutrient groups
- Trace food through digestion
- Link nutrition to energy
