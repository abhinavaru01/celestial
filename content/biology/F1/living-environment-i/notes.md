# Living Things & Their Environment I

> Food chains and webs as energy flow, and how organisms depend on each other.

## Energy flows through ecosystems

Food chains trace energy from producers (plants) to consumers. Energy is lost at each step, so chains are short — an ecological version of energy accounting.

## Interdependence

Organisms depend on each other and their environment; removing one affects many, shown by food webs linking multiple chains.

## What you should be able to do

- Construct food chains and webs
- Explain energy flow
- Describe interdependence
