# Living Things & Their Environment I

> [!intro] Food chains and webs as energy flow, and how organisms depend on each other.

## Energy flows through ecosystems

Food chains trace energy from producers (plants) to consumers. Energy is lost at each step, so chains are short — an ecological version of energy accounting.

## Interdependence

Organisms depend on each other and their environment; removing one affects many, shown by food webs linking multiple chains.

```formula Key facts
Food-chain arrows point in the direction of energy flow
Every chain begins with a producer
Energy is lost as heat at each level
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** In grass → grasshopper → frog, what is the producer and the primary consumer?
> >
> > **Solution.** Grass is the producer; the grasshopper (which eats the producer) is the primary consumer.
