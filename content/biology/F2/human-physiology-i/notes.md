# Human Physiology I

> Respiration, circulation and transport as flow systems in the body.

## Breathing and respiration

Breathing brings oxygen in and removes carbon dioxide; cellular respiration then releases energy from glucose. The two are related but distinct.

## Circulation as transport

The heart pumps blood through vessels to deliver oxygen and nutrients and remove waste — a transport network serving every cell.

## What you should be able to do

- Describe gas exchange and breathing
- Explain the circulatory system
- Relate transport to cell needs
