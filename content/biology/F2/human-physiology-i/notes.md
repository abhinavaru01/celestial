# Human Physiology I

> [!intro] Respiration, circulation and transport as flow systems in the body.

## Breathing and respiration

Breathing brings oxygen in and removes carbon dioxide; cellular respiration then releases energy from glucose. The two are related but distinct.

## Circulation as transport

The heart pumps blood through vessels to deliver oxygen and nutrients and remove waste — a transport network serving every cell.

```formula Key facts
Arteries carry blood away from the heart; veins toward it
Breathing = gas exchange; respiration = energy release
Glucose + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + energy
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Why do arteries have thick, muscular walls?
> >
> > **Solution.** To withstand the high pressure of blood pumped directly from the heart, and to help maintain that pressure.
