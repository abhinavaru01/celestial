# Health & Disease

> Pathogens, how disease spreads, immunity and prevention.

## Pathogens

Bacteria, viruses, fungi and parasites cause infectious disease, spreading by air, water, contact or vectors. Understanding the route guides prevention.

## Immunity

The immune system recognises and destroys pathogens; vaccines train it in advance. Hygiene and vaccination are the front line of prevention.

## What you should be able to do

- Identify types of pathogen
- Explain how disease spreads
- Describe immunity and prevention
