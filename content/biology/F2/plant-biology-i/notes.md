# Plant Biology I

> Photosynthesis as energy capture, and transport in plants.

## Photosynthesis

Plants capture light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water, releasing oxygen. This is the entry point of energy into most food chains.

## Plant transport

Xylem carries water up from roots; phloem carries sugars around the plant. Leaves are adapted (broad, thin, full of chloroplasts) to capture light.

## What you should be able to do

- Describe photosynthesis
- Explain transport in plants
- Relate leaves to their function
