# Reproduction

> Asexual and sexual reproduction, and the human reproductive system.

## Two strategies

Asexual reproduction makes identical offspring from one parent (fast, no variation). Sexual reproduction combines two parents' genes, producing variation — the raw material for evolution.

## Human reproduction

Sexual reproduction in humans joins sperm and egg; the resulting variation is why siblings differ. This links directly to later genetics.

## What you should be able to do

- Compare asexual and sexual reproduction
- Describe human reproduction
- Explain variation from sexual reproduction
