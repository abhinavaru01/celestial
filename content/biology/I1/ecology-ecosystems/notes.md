# Ecology & Ecosystems

> [!intro] Energy pyramids, nutrient cycles and population basics.

## Energy pyramids

Energy decreases up trophic levels (only ~10% passes on), so top predators are few. This explains pyramid shapes and short food chains.

## Cycles

Matter (carbon, nitrogen, water) cycles through ecosystems, unlike energy which flows through and is lost. Decomposers return nutrients to the soil.

```formula Key facts
~10% of energy passes to the next trophic level
Matter cycles; energy flows and is lost
Decomposers recycle nutrients
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** If producers capture 10000 kJ, roughly how much reaches a secondary consumer?
> >
> > **Solution.** ~10% per level: producer 10000 → primary 1000 → secondary ~100 kJ.
