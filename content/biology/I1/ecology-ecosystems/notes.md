# Ecology & Ecosystems

> Energy pyramids, nutrient cycles and population basics.

## Energy pyramids

Energy decreases up trophic levels (only ~10% passes on), so top predators are few. This explains pyramid shapes and short food chains.

## Cycles

Matter (carbon, nitrogen, water) cycles through ecosystems, unlike energy which flows through and is lost. Decomposers return nutrients to the soil.

## What you should be able to do

- Interpret energy pyramids
- Describe nutrient cycles
- Explain population change
