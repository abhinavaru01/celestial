# Enzymes

> [!intro] Enzymes as biological catalysts, and the factors that affect their activity.

## Biological catalysts

Enzymes speed up reactions by lowering activation energy, binding substrates at an active site (lock-and-key). They are not used up — reusing the catalyst idea from chemistry.

## Sensitive to conditions

Temperature and pH affect enzyme shape; too much denatures them permanently. Each enzyme has an optimum, explaining why body temperature matters.

```formula Key facts
Enzymes lower activation energy (biological catalysts)
Each has an optimum temperature and pH
High heat / extreme pH denatures (permanent)
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Why does an enzyme stop working when boiled?
> >
> > **Solution.** Heat denatures it — the active site changes shape permanently, so the substrate no longer fits.
