# Enzymes

> Enzymes as biological catalysts, and the factors that affect their activity.

## Biological catalysts

Enzymes speed up reactions by lowering activation energy, binding substrates at an active site (lock-and-key). They are not used up — reusing the catalyst idea from chemistry.

## Sensitive to conditions

Temperature and pH affect enzyme shape; too much denatures them permanently. Each enzyme has an optimum, explaining why body temperature matters.

## What you should be able to do

- Explain enzyme action
- Describe factors affecting enzymes
- Relate enzymes to reaction rate
