# Human Physiology II

> [!intro] Nervous and endocrine coordination, and homeostasis as feedback control.

## Two control systems

The nervous system sends fast electrical signals; the endocrine system sends slower, longer-lasting hormones. Together they coordinate the body.

## Homeostasis

The body keeps internal conditions stable (temperature, glucose) using negative feedback — deviations trigger corrections, like a thermostat.

```formula Key facts
Nervous control: fast, brief (electrical)
Hormonal control: slower, longer (chemical)
Negative feedback opposes the change (homeostasis)
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Blood glucose rises after a meal. How does negative feedback restore it?
> >
> > **Solution.** The pancreas releases insulin, which makes cells take up glucose, lowering blood glucose back to normal.
