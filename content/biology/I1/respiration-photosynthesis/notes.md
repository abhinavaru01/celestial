# Respiration & Photosynthesis (Biochemical)

> The energy budgets of respiration and photosynthesis, quantitatively.

## Two complementary processes

Photosynthesis stores light energy in glucose; respiration releases it. They are near-opposites, and together drive energy flow through ecosystems.

## Aerobic vs anaerobic

Aerobic respiration (with oxygen) releases much more energy than anaerobic. The equations reveal the reactants and products precisely.

## What you should be able to do

- Write respiration and photosynthesis equations
- Compare the two processes
- Relate them to energy flow
