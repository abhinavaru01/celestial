# Respiration & Photosynthesis (Biochemical)

> [!intro] The energy budgets of respiration and photosynthesis, quantitatively.

## Two complementary processes

Photosynthesis stores light energy in glucose; respiration releases it. They are near-opposites, and together drive energy flow through ecosystems.

## Aerobic vs anaerobic

Aerobic respiration (with oxygen) releases much more energy than anaerobic. The equations reveal the reactants and products precisely.

```formula Key facts
Aerobic: glucose + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + energy
Anaerobic releases less energy
Photosynthesis ≈ the reverse of respiration
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Why does aerobic respiration release more energy than anaerobic?
> >
> > **Solution.** With oxygen, glucose is fully broken down to CO₂ and water; without it, glucose is only partially broken down.
