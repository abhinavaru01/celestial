# Biotechnology

> [!intro] Recombinant DNA, PCR, applications and ethics — linked to argumentation.

## Engineering DNA

Recombinant DNA technology cuts and pastes genes between organisms; PCR amplifies tiny DNA samples. These tools power medicine, agriculture and forensics.

## Ethics

Powerful tools raise questions — GM crops, gene editing, privacy. Reasoning through them connects to the argumentation skills built in English.

```formula Key facts
PCR amplifies (copies) DNA
Restriction enzymes cut DNA at specific sites
Recombinant DNA joins genes from different sources
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Why is PCR useful in forensic science?
> >
> > **Solution.** It amplifies tiny DNA samples from a crime scene into enough copies to analyse and match to a suspect.

## What you should be able to do

- Describe genetic engineering tools
- Explain PCR
- Weigh ethical issues
