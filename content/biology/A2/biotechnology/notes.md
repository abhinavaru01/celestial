# Biotechnology

> Recombinant DNA, PCR, applications and ethics — linked to argumentation.

## Engineering DNA

Recombinant DNA technology cuts and pastes genes between organisms; PCR amplifies tiny DNA samples. These tools power medicine, agriculture and forensics.

## Ethics

Powerful tools raise questions — GM crops, gene editing, privacy. Reasoning through them connects to the argumentation skills built in English.

## What you should be able to do

- Describe genetic engineering tools
- Explain PCR
- Weigh ethical issues
