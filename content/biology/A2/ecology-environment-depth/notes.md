# Ecology & Environment at Depth

> [!intro] Biodiversity, conservation and human impact — data-driven, reusing statistics.

## Biodiversity and impact

Biodiversity underpins ecosystem stability; human activity (habitat loss, pollution, climate change) threatens it. Data and statistics quantify these trends.

## Conservation

Conservation weighs evidence and trade-offs to protect species and habitats — an applied, data-driven, and ethical challenge.

```formula Key facts
Biodiversity = genetic + species + ecosystem diversity
Human impacts: habitat loss, pollution, climate change
Use data/statistics to quantify change
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Why does habitat loss reduce biodiversity so strongly?
> >
> > **Solution.** It removes the niches many species depend on, causing local extinctions and breaking food webs, which cascades to more losses.
