# Ecology & Environment at Depth

> Biodiversity, conservation and human impact — data-driven, reusing statistics.

## Biodiversity and impact

Biodiversity underpins ecosystem stability; human activity (habitat loss, pollution, climate change) threatens it. Data and statistics quantify these trends.

## Conservation

Conservation weighs evidence and trade-offs to protect species and habitats — an applied, data-driven, and ethical challenge.

## What you should be able to do

- Assess biodiversity
- Evaluate human impact
- Propose conservation strategies
