# Evolution at Depth

> Speciation, molecular evidence and phylogenetics.

## Speciation

New species form when populations become reproductively isolated and diverge — often by geographic separation. This explains life's branching diversity.

## Molecular phylogenetics

Comparing DNA sequences reconstructs evolutionary relationships as trees. More similar sequences mean more recent common ancestors.

## What you should be able to do

- Explain speciation
- Use molecular evidence
- Read phylogenetic trees
