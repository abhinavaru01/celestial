# Genetics II

> [!intro] Linkage, mutation, genetic disorders and population genetics (Hardy-Weinberg).

## Beyond simple inheritance

Genes on the same chromosome are linked; mutations change DNA, sometimes causing disorders. Real inheritance is richer than single-gene ratios.

## Population genetics

Hardy-Weinberg predicts allele frequencies in a stable population — reusing probability and algebra to study evolution quantitatively.

```formula Key facts
Linked genes are inherited together
Hardy–Weinberg: p² + 2pq + q² = 1, p + q = 1
Mutations are the source of new alleles
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** In a population, the recessive phenotype frequency q² = 0.16. Find q and p.
> >
> > **Solution.** q = √0.16 = 0.4. p = 1 − q = 0.6.
