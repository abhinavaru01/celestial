# Genetics II

> Linkage, mutation, genetic disorders and population genetics (Hardy-Weinberg).

## Beyond simple inheritance

Genes on the same chromosome are linked; mutations change DNA, sometimes causing disorders. Real inheritance is richer than single-gene ratios.

## Population genetics

Hardy-Weinberg predicts allele frequencies in a stable population — reusing probability and algebra to study evolution quantitatively.

## What you should be able to do

- Explain linkage and mutation
- Analyse genetic disorders
- Apply Hardy-Weinberg
