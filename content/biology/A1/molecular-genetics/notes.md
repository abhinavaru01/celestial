# Molecular Genetics

> Transcription, translation and gene regulation in detail.

## Gene expression

Transcription copies a gene to mRNA; translation reads mRNA codons (three bases) to build a protein from amino acids. The genetic code maps codons to amino acids.

## Regulation

Cells switch genes on and off so the right proteins are made at the right time — why a nerve cell and a skin cell differ despite identical DNA.

## What you should be able to do

- Detail transcription and translation
- Explain the genetic code
- Describe gene regulation
