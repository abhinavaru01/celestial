# Cell Biology at Depth

> [!intro] Membrane transport, the cell cycle, and cell division (mitosis and meiosis).

## Cell cycle and division

Cells grow and divide in a controlled cycle. Mitosis makes two identical cells (growth, repair); meiosis makes four genetically varied gametes (reproduction).

## Why meiosis matters

Meiosis halves the chromosome number and shuffles genes, creating the variation that Genetics I described. It is the molecular source of inherited diversity.

```formula Key facts
Mitosis: 2 identical diploid cells (growth/repair)
Meiosis: 4 varied haploid gametes
Meiosis halves chromosome number and shuffles genes
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Why must gametes be made by meiosis, not mitosis?
> >
> > **Solution.** Meiosis halves the chromosome number so that fertilisation restores the normal diploid number, and it creates genetic variation.
