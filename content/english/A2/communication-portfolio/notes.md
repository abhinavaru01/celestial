# Communication Portfolio

> [!intro] A body of polished writing across genres — the capstone strand.

## Range and control

The portfolio gathers a learner's best work across genres — narrative, argument, analysis, technical — showing versatility and command of the language.

## Revision

Polish comes from drafting and revising. Reflecting on feedback and revising deliberately is what turns competent writing into excellent writing.

```formula Key techniques
Show range across genres
Revise deliberately using feedback
Reflect on what each piece demonstrates
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** What turns competent writing into excellent writing?
> >
> > **Solution.** Deliberate revision — drafting, acting on feedback, and refining word choice, structure and clarity.

## What you should be able to do

- Produce polished writing in several genres
- Reflect on and revise work
- Demonstrate range and control
