# Critical Reasoning & Comprehension (Exam Level)

> [!intro] Dense unseen passages under time — serving aptitude sections of entrance exams.

## Reading under pressure

Exam comprehension demands fast, accurate reading of unfamiliar dense text — finding the main idea, inferences and the author's stance quickly.

## Critical reasoning

Aptitude sections test evaluating arguments: spotting assumptions, conclusions and flaws under time. It applies all the argument skills at speed.

```formula Key techniques
Skim for structure, then read for the question
Every answer must be supported by the passage
Identify conclusion and assumptions fast
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Under time pressure, what is the first move on a dense passage?
> >
> > **Solution.** Skim for the structure and main idea, then read closely only the parts the questions target.

## What you should be able to do

- Read dense passages quickly and accurately
- Answer inference questions under time
- Evaluate arguments rapidly
