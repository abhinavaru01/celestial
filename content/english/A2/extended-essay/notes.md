# The Extended Essay / Independent Research Writing

> A sustained, sourced, argued piece — the writing capstone.

## Independent research

The extended essay is a self-directed investigation of a focused question, argued over thousands of words with rigorous sourcing — the culmination of every writing skill.

## Sustained argument

Holding one argument across a long piece demands planning, clear sections and disciplined citation. It mirrors the capstones in other subjects.

## What you should be able to do

- Plan an independent research essay
- Sustain a long argument
- Cite and synthesise rigorously
