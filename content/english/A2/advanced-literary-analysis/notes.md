# Advanced Literary Analysis

> Poetry, drama and the novel analysed at examination depth.

## Depth across genres

Advanced analysis reads poetry, drama and prose with equal rigour, attending to form-specific techniques (metre, staging, narration) and their effects.

## Layered meaning

Sophisticated texts carry multiple, sometimes conflicting meanings. Strong essays argue a nuanced reading supported by close evidence.

## What you should be able to do

- Analyse across genres
- Handle complex, layered texts
- Write exam-level literary essays
