# Rhetoric & Style at Depth

> [!intro] Voice, syntax variety and precision in advanced writing.

## Voice and syntax

Mature writing has a recognisable voice, built from word choice and sentence rhythm. Deliberately varying sentence structure controls emphasis and pace.

## Precision

Precise word choice removes ambiguity and strengthens argument. Every word earns its place — the mark of advanced style.

```formula Key techniques
Vary sentence length for emphasis and rhythm
Precise words remove ambiguity
Read aloud to hear your voice
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** How can a short sentence after long ones add power?
> >
> > **Solution.** The contrast in rhythm makes the short sentence land hard, drawing the reader’s attention to it. Like this.

## What you should be able to do

- Develop a distinctive voice
- Vary syntax for effect
- Write with precision
