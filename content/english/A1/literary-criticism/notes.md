# Literary Criticism

> [!intro] Critical lenses and close analysis of complex texts.

## Critical lenses

Different lenses (historical, feminist, psychological) reveal different meanings in a text. A lens is a deliberate perspective that focuses analysis.

## Close analysis

Sophisticated criticism combines a lens with close attention to language, structure and context, arguing an interpretation rather than asserting it.

```formula Key techniques
A critical lens focuses interpretation (historical, feminist, etc.)
Argue interpretation with textual evidence
Context shapes meaning
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** What does a "historical lens" add to reading a novel?
> >
> > **Solution.** It interprets the text through its time period, revealing how contemporary events and values shape its meaning.

## What you should be able to do

- Apply critical lenses
- Analyse complex texts closely
- Support interpretation with theory
