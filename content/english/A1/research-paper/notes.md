# The Research Paper

> [!intro] Thesis, literature synthesis and sustained argument across a long paper.

## Research argument

A research paper argues an original thesis supported by evidence from multiple sources, synthesised rather than listed. It scales argument and synthesis to full length.

## Sustained structure

Long papers need a roadmap: sections that each advance the thesis, with citations throughout. This connects to any subject's capstone.

```formula Key techniques
Argue an original thesis (not just report)
Synthesise sources into your argument
Cite consistently throughout
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** What makes a thesis "arguable" rather than descriptive?
> >
> > **Solution.** It takes a position that could be debated and defended with evidence, rather than merely stating an agreed fact.

## What you should be able to do

- Develop a research thesis
- Synthesise a literature base
- Sustain an argument at length
