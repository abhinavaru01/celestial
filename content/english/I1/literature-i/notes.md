# Literature I

> Short stories and drama: analysing theme and character.

## Theme

A theme is the underlying idea a story explores (justice, growing up). It is inferred from events and characters, not stated outright.

## Character

Characters reveal themselves through actions, words and change over the story. Strong analysis quotes the text to support each claim.

## What you should be able to do

- Analyse theme
- Trace character development
- Support analysis with textual evidence
