# Research & Citation Basics

> [!intro] Finding, evaluating and attributing sources honestly.

## Finding good sources

Reliable research favours credible, current, evidence-based sources. Evaluating them reuses the source-evaluation skills from media analysis.

## Attribution

Citing sources credits others' ideas and lets readers verify them. Failing to cite is plagiarism — an ethical and academic failure.

```formula Key techniques
Prefer credible, current, evidence-based sources
Cite every idea that is not your own
Uncited use of others’ ideas = plagiarism
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** You paraphrase a fact from a website. Do you still cite it?
> >
> > **Solution.** Yes — paraphrasing does not remove the need to credit the source; only common knowledge is exempt.

## What you should be able to do

- Find and evaluate sources
- Cite sources correctly
- Avoid plagiarism
