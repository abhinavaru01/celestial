# Analysing Non-Fiction & Media

> [!intro] Rhetoric, bias and source evaluation — critical reading of real-world texts.

## Rhetoric

Writers persuade using appeals to logic (logos), emotion (pathos) and credibility (ethos). Recognising these reveals how a text tries to move you.

## Bias and reliability

Every source has a perspective; evaluating who wrote it, why, and with what evidence separates trustworthy information from spin — essential for science and citizenship.

```formula Key techniques
Ethos (credibility), Pathos (emotion), Logos (logic)
Judge a source by author and purpose
Check the evidence behind claims
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** An advert says "9 out of 10 dentists recommend it." Which appeal, and what to check?
> >
> > **Solution.** Ethos (authority of dentists). Check the sample size, who funded it, and whether the claim is verifiable.

## What you should be able to do

- Identify rhetorical techniques
- Detect bias
- Evaluate source reliability
