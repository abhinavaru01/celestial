# The Persuasive / Argumentative Essay

> [!intro] Writing an essay that argues a position with evidence and reasoning.

## Arguable thesis

A persuasive essay takes a clear, arguable position — one a reasonable person could dispute — and defends it with evidence and logic.

## Building the case

Each paragraph advances one reason, supported and explained. The strongest essays anticipate the reader's doubts (setting up counterargument, next).

```formula Key techniques
Thesis must be arguable
Reason + evidence + explanation per paragraph
Anticipate objections
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Is "Homework should be reduced" a good persuasive thesis?
> >
> > **Solution.** Yes — it is arguable (a reasonable person could disagree), so it can be defended with evidence and reasoning.

## What you should be able to do

- Build a persuasive thesis
- Support with evidence and reasoning
- Structure a convincing argument
