# Literature II

> The novel: sustained analysis in an extended essay.

## Reading a whole work

Analysing a novel means tracing how theme, character and technique develop across hundreds of pages — sustaining an argument over length.

## The extended essay

A longer analytical essay demands a clear thesis, structured sections, and consistent evidence — scaling all earlier writing skills up.

## What you should be able to do

- Analyse a whole novel
- Trace development across a text
- Write an extended literary essay
