# Comparative Analysis

> [!intro] Synthesising and comparing multiple texts.

## Comparing meaningfully

Comparison examines how two texts treat a shared theme, technique or idea — noting both similarities and differences, not just listing features of each.

## Synthesis

Synthesis weaves sources together into a new, coherent understanding rather than summarising each in turn — a key academic skill.

```formula Key techniques
Compare point-by-point, not text-by-text
Synthesise into a new understanding
Note both similarities and differences
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Two poems treat war differently. How should you structure the comparison?
> >
> > **Solution.** Point-by-point — compare both poems on each aspect (imagery, tone, form) in turn, rather than describing each poem separately.

## What you should be able to do

- Compare texts on shared aspects
- Synthesise ideas across sources
- Structure a comparative essay
