# Comparative Analysis

> Synthesising and comparing multiple texts.

## Comparing meaningfully

Comparison examines how two texts treat a shared theme, technique or idea — noting both similarities and differences, not just listing features of each.

## Synthesis

Synthesis weaves sources together into a new, coherent understanding rather than summarising each in turn — a key academic skill.

## What you should be able to do

- Compare texts on shared aspects
- Synthesise ideas across sources
- Structure a comparative essay
