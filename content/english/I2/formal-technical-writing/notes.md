# Formal & Technical Writing

> Reports and lab-style write-ups — a skill shared with the science portfolios.

## Technical clarity

Reports and lab write-ups value precision and objectivity over flair: clear structure, defined terms, and exactly what was done and found. This directly serves science practicals.

## Structure

Formal documents use headings, logical order and concise language so a reader can find and trust information quickly.

## What you should be able to do

- Write clear formal reports
- Structure technical documents
- Write with precision and objectivity
