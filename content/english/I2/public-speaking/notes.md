# Public Speaking & Structured Discussion

> Speaking clearly, listening well, and structuring a discussion or debate.

## Structuring speech

A good talk has a clear opening, structured points and a memorable close — like an essay, but delivered. Signposting helps listeners follow.

## Discussion and delivery

Effective discussion means listening actively and responding to what was actually said. Delivery — pace, volume, eye contact — carries the message.

## What you should be able to do

- Structure a spoken argument
- Listen and respond constructively
- Use delivery techniques
