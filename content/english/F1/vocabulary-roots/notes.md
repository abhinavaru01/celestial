# Vocabulary by Roots

> [!intro] Building vocabulary through Greek and Latin roots, prefixes and suffixes.

## Words have parts

Many English words are built from Greek/Latin roots plus prefixes and suffixes. Knowing "bio" (life) and "logy" (study) unlocks "biology" and dozens more — vocabulary that compounds.

## Inferring meaning

Faced with an unknown word, breaking it into known parts often reveals the meaning without a dictionary — a far more powerful strategy than memorising lists.

```formula Key techniques
Root "aqua" = water, "bio" = life, "port" = carry
Prefixes un-/in-/dis- often negate
Break unknown words into known parts
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Deduce the meaning of "biography" from its roots.
> >
> > **Solution.** "bio" (life) + "graph" (write) → a written account of a life.

## What you should be able to do

- Decode words using roots and affixes
- Infer meaning of unfamiliar words
- Build word families
