# Paragraph Architecture

> Building coherent paragraphs: topic sentence, support and coherence.

## Paragraph structure

A paragraph makes one point: a topic sentence states it, supporting sentences develop it with evidence or detail, and a closing sentence rounds it off.

## Coherence

Linking words (however, therefore, for example) guide the reader through the logic. Coherent paragraphs are the building blocks of every essay.

## What you should be able to do

- Write a clear topic sentence
- Support it with details
- Achieve coherence and flow
