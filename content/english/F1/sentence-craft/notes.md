# Sentence Craft

> Grammar as a tool for clarity: sentence types and combining sentences well.

## Sentences as tools

A sentence expresses a complete thought. Varying simple, compound and complex sentences controls rhythm and clarity — grammar serves meaning, not the other way round.

## Combining sentences

Joining short, choppy sentences with conjunctions creates flow and shows how ideas relate (cause, contrast, sequence).

## What you should be able to do

- Identify sentence types
- Combine sentences for flow
- Use grammar for clarity
