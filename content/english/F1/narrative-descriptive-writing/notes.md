# Narrative & Descriptive Writing

> Telling a story and painting a scene with vivid, purposeful detail.

## Story shape

Narratives have a beginning, rising action, climax and resolution. Structure keeps a reader engaged and gives events meaning.

## Show, don't tell

Vivid, sensory detail lets readers experience a scene rather than being told about it. "Her hands trembled" beats "she was nervous".

## What you should be able to do

- Structure a narrative
- Use sensory description
- Show rather than tell
