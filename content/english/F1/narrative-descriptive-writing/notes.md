# Narrative & Descriptive Writing

> [!intro] Telling a story and painting a scene with vivid, purposeful detail.

## Story shape

Narratives have a beginning, rising action, climax and resolution. Structure keeps a reader engaged and gives events meaning.

## Show, don't tell

Vivid, sensory detail lets readers experience a scene rather than being told about it. "Her hands trembled" beats "she was nervous".

```formula Key techniques
Show, don’t tell (use sensory action)
Story shape: setup → rising action → climax → resolution
Choose purposeful detail
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Rewrite "He was scared" to show it.
> >
> > **Solution.** "His hands trembled and his breath caught as the door creaked open." — the emotion is shown through action.

## What you should be able to do

- Structure a narrative
- Use sensory description
- Show rather than tell
