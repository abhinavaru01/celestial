# Close Reading of Prose & Poetry

> Analysing figurative language, tone and structure in texts.

## Figurative language

Metaphor, simile, personification and imagery carry meaning beyond the literal. Naming and interpreting them is the core of literary analysis.

## Tone and structure

Tone is the writer's attitude; structure is how a text is organised. Both shape how a reader feels and understands — details are chosen, not accidental.

## What you should be able to do

- Identify figurative language
- Analyse tone and mood
- Explain how structure shapes meaning
