# Structured Exposition

> Writing the multi-paragraph explanatory essay.

## Essay structure

An explanatory essay opens with a thesis, develops it in body paragraphs (each one point), and closes by drawing the threads together — a scalable structure for any topic.

## Unity and flow

Every paragraph serves the thesis; transitions connect them. This is paragraph architecture applied at essay scale.

## What you should be able to do

- Plan a multi-paragraph essay
- Write clear body paragraphs
- Introduce and conclude effectively
