# Structured Exposition

> [!intro] Writing the multi-paragraph explanatory essay.

## Essay structure

An explanatory essay opens with a thesis, develops it in body paragraphs (each one point), and closes by drawing the threads together — a scalable structure for any topic.

## Unity and flow

Every paragraph serves the thesis; transitions connect them. This is paragraph architecture applied at essay scale.

```formula Key techniques
Introduction states the thesis
One point per body paragraph, tied to the thesis
Transitions link paragraphs
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** What belongs in the introduction of an explanatory essay?
> >
> > **Solution.** A clear thesis stating the essay’s central point, plus a brief signpost of the main supporting ideas.

## What you should be able to do

- Plan a multi-paragraph essay
- Write clear body paragraphs
- Introduce and conclude effectively
