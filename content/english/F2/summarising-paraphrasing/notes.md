# Summarising & Paraphrasing

> Compressing and restating ideas precisely — a skill that serves every subject.

## Precise compression

A summary captures the key ideas of a text in far fewer words. Doing it well means separating the essential from the incidental — the same skill as taking good notes in science.

## Paraphrasing

Paraphrasing restates an idea in your own words at similar length, preserving meaning. It shows understanding and avoids plagiarism.

## What you should be able to do

- Write accurate summaries
- Paraphrase without changing meaning
- Distinguish summary from paraphrase
