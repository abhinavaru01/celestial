# Bonding I

> Ionic, covalent and metallic bonding, and electron-dot structures.

## Atoms bond for stable shells

Atoms gain, lose or share electrons to reach full outer shells. Metals + non-metals transfer electrons (ionic); non-metals share (covalent); metals pool electrons (metallic).

## Bonding sets properties

Ionic compounds form lattices with high melting points; covalent molecules are often low-melting; metals conduct because their electrons are free.

## What you should be able to do

- Distinguish ionic, covalent, metallic bonds
- Draw electron-dot structures
- Relate bonding to properties
