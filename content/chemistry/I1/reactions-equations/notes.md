# Reactions & Equations Deepened

> [!intro] Reaction types, net ionic equations, oxidation numbers and the essentials of redox.

## Types of reaction

Combination, decomposition, displacement, double displacement (precipitation/neutralisation). The type predicts the products.

## Redox by electron transfer

Oxidation is loss of electrons, reduction is gain (OIL RIG). Oxidation numbers track it: the species whose oxidation number rises is oxidised; the oxidising agent is itself reduced.

```formula Key formulas
OIL RIG: Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons)
Oxidation number ↑ = oxidised; ↓ = reduced
Net ionic equation: cancel spectator ions
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** In Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu, which species is oxidised?
> >
> > **Solution.** Zn: 0 → +2 (loses electrons) → oxidised (reducing agent). Cu²⁺: +2 → 0 (gains electrons) → reduced.

## What you should be able to do

- Write net ionic equations
- Assign oxidation numbers and identify redox
