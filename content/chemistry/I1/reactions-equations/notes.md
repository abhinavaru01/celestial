# Reactions & Equations Deepened

> Reaction types, ionic equations and an introduction to redox.

## Types of reaction

Reactions fall into families: combination, decomposition, displacement, and double displacement. Recognising the type predicts the products.

## Redox intro

Oxidation is loss of electrons, reduction is gain (OIL RIG). Many reactions transfer electrons — the basis of batteries and corrosion.

## What you should be able to do

- Classify reaction types
- Write ionic equations
- Identify oxidation and reduction
