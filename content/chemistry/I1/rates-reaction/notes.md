# Rates of Reaction

> What speeds reactions up — concentration, temperature, surface area, catalysts — with rate graphs.

## Collision theory

Reactions happen when particles collide with enough energy. Anything that increases collision frequency or energy speeds the reaction: higher concentration, temperature, surface area, or a catalyst.

## Rate graphs

Plotting product formed vs time gives a curve whose slope is the rate — steep at first, levelling as reactants run out. This is rate-as-slope, shared with physics and calculus.

## What you should be able to do

- Identify factors affecting rate
- Interpret rate graphs
- Explain rate using collision theory
