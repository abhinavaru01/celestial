# Solutions & Concentration

> Molarity, dilution, and expressing concentration — the mole concept reused.

## Concentration

Molarity is moles of solute per litre of solution — again a rate/ratio. It links the mole concept to real solutions used in the lab.

## Dilution

Adding solvent lowers concentration but keeps the moles of solute fixed: C₁V₁ = C₂V₂. This is inverse proportion in action.

## What you should be able to do

- Calculate molarity
- Perform dilution calculations
- Relate concentration to moles
