# Chemical Thermodynamics

> Enthalpy, entropy and Gibbs free energy — predicting whether reactions happen.

## Enthalpy and entropy

Enthalpy (ΔH) tracks heat; entropy (ΔS) tracks disorder. Reactions are favoured by releasing energy and increasing disorder.

## Gibbs free energy

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS decides spontaneity: negative ΔG means the reaction proceeds. This unifies energy and disorder, sharing a spine with physics thermodynamics.

## What you should be able to do

- Use enthalpy and entropy
- Apply Gibbs free energy
- Predict spontaneity
