# Periodic Properties & s/p-Block

> [!intro] Trends in atomic radius, ionisation energy, electron affinity and electronegativity, explained by nuclear charge and shielding.

## Trends from effective nuclear charge

Across a period, more protons pull the same shell in → atoms shrink, ionisation energy and electronegativity rise. Down a group, extra shells and shielding → atoms grow, ionisation energy falls.

## Block chemistry

The s-block (reactive metals, +1/+2 ions) and p-block (non-metals, metalloids, varied) show patterns explained by valence configuration.

```formula Key formulas
Across a period →: radius ↓, ionisation energy ↑, electronegativity ↑
Down a group ↓: radius ↑, ionisation energy ↓
Effective nuclear charge ≈ protons − shielding
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Which has the larger atomic radius: Na or Cl? Why?
> >
> > **Solution.** Na. Both are Period 3, but Cl has more protons pulling the same shell in (higher effective nuclear charge), so Cl is smaller — radius decreases across a period.
