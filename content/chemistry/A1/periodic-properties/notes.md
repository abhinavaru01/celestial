# Periodic Properties & s/p-Block

> Trends in atomic radius, ionisation energy and electronegativity, and s- and p-block chemistry.

## Periodic trends

Atomic radius decreases across a period and increases down a group; ionisation energy and electronegativity do the opposite. These follow from nuclear charge and shielding.

## Block chemistry

The s-block (reactive metals) and p-block (varied non-metals and metalloids) show patterns explained by their outer configurations.

## What you should be able to do

- Explain periodic trends
- Relate trends to electron structure
- Survey s- and p-block behaviour
