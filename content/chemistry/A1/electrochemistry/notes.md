# Electrochemistry

> Galvanic and electrolytic cells, the Nernst equation, and electrolysis.

## Cells from redox

A galvanic cell turns a spontaneous redox reaction into electricity; oxidation at the anode, reduction at the cathode. Cell voltage comes from electrode potentials.

## Electrolysis

Electrolytic cells use electricity to drive non-spontaneous reactions — extracting metals, plating. The amount deposited follows Faraday's laws (mole-charge ratios).

## What you should be able to do

- Describe galvanic and electrolytic cells
- Use standard electrode potentials
- Explain electrolysis
