# Electrochemistry

> [!intro] Galvanic and electrolytic cells, cell potentials, the Nernst equation and Faraday's laws of electrolysis.

## Cells from redox

A galvanic cell turns a spontaneous redox reaction into electricity: oxidation at the anode, reduction at the cathode. E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode; positive → spontaneous.

## Electrolysis and Faraday

An electrolytic cell uses electricity to drive a non-spontaneous reaction. Amount deposited follows Faraday: moles of electrons = charge ÷ F, F ≈ 96500 C/mol.

```formula Key formulas
E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode  (>0 → spontaneous)
ΔG° = −nFE°cell
Faraday: moles e⁻ = Q/F,  Q = I×t,  F ≈ 96500 C/mol
Nernst: E = E° − (RT/nF) ln Q
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A current of 2 A flows for 965 s through molten NaCl. How many moles of electrons pass?
> >
> > **Solution.** Q = I×t = 2 × 965 = 1930 C. Moles e⁻ = Q/F = 1930/96500 = 0.02 mol — deposits 0.02 mol Na at the cathode.
