# Ionic Equilibrium

> pH calculations, buffers, and the solubility product.

## pH quantitatively

pH = −log[H⁺], so acidity is a logarithm of ion concentration. Strong acids fully dissociate; weak acids only partly, needing equilibrium methods.

## Buffers and Ksp

Buffers resist pH change using a weak acid and its salt. The solubility product Ksp governs how much of a sparingly soluble salt dissolves.

## What you should be able to do

- Calculate pH of acids/bases
- Explain buffers
- Use the solubility product Ksp
