# Chemical Kinetics

> Rate laws, reaction order and the Arrhenius equation — how fast and why.

## Rate laws

The rate law relates reaction rate to reactant concentrations raised to their orders (found by experiment, not from the equation). Order reveals the mechanism.

## Arrhenius

Rate constants rise with temperature and fall with activation energy (Arrhenius). Taking logs turns this into a straight line — reusing logarithms from maths.

## What you should be able to do

- Write and use rate laws
- Determine reaction order
- Apply the Arrhenius relationship
