# Gas Laws & Kinetic Theory

> Boyle, Charles and the ideal gas equation — the topic shared with physics.

## Gas laws

Pressure, volume and temperature are linked. Boyle (P↕V at fixed T) and Charles (V↕T at fixed P) combine into PV = nRT, connecting to the mole concept.

## Particle picture

A gas is fast-moving particles colliding with walls; temperature is their average kinetic energy. The macroscopic laws follow from this microscopic behaviour.

## What you should be able to do

- Apply the gas laws
- Use PV = nRT
- Relate gas behaviour to particle motion
