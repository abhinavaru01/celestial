# Atomic Structure II

> [!intro] Electron configuration, subshells and quantum shells — the origin of the periodic table's shape.

## Electrons fill by energy

Electrons occupy shells and subshells (s, p, d) in order of increasing energy (aufbau). Configuration like 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s¹ (Na) explains an element's group and reactivity.

## Valence controls chemistry

The outer-shell configuration determines bonding. Noble gases have full shells (unreactive); Group 1 has one easily-lost outer electron.

```formula Key formulas
Shell capacity = 2n² electrons (n = shell number)
Filling order (aufbau): 1s 2s 2p 3s 3p 4s 3d ...
Valence electrons = electrons in the outermost shell
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Write the electron configuration of sodium (Z = 11) and give its group.
> >
> > **Solution.** 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s¹. One valence electron in shell 3 → Group 1, Period 3. That 3s electron is easily lost to form Na⁺.
