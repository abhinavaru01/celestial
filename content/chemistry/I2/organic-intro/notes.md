# Introductory Organic Chemistry

> Hydrocarbons, functional groups and naming — the chemistry of carbon.

## Carbon's versatility

Carbon forms four bonds and chains with itself, giving millions of compounds. Hydrocarbons (alkanes, alkenes) are the simplest families.

## Functional groups

A functional group (like −OH, −COOH) is the reactive part that defines a molecule's chemistry. Naming follows systematic rules based on chain length and group.

## What you should be able to do

- Recognise hydrocarbons and functional groups
- Name simple organic molecules
- Explain why carbon forms so many compounds
