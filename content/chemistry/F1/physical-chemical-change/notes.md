# Physical vs Chemical Change

> Telling physical from chemical changes, evidence of reaction, and conservation of mass.

## Two kinds of change

A physical change alters form but not the substance (melting, dissolving). A chemical change makes new substances (burning, rusting), often signalled by colour change, gas, heat or a precipitate.

## Mass is conserved

In any chemical reaction, atoms are only rearranged — total mass before equals total mass after. This accounting principle underlies balancing equations later.

## What you should be able to do

- Distinguish physical and chemical changes
- Identify signs of a chemical reaction
- Apply conservation of mass
