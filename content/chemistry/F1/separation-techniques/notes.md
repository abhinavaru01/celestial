# Separation Techniques

> [!intro] Filtration, evaporation, distillation and chromatography — separating mixtures by exploiting differences in physical properties.

## Separate by difference

Mixtures separate because components differ — particle size (filtration), boiling point (distillation), or affinity for a moving vs stationary phase (chromatography).

## Chromatography and Rf

In paper chromatography, components travel different distances. The retention factor Rf = (distance moved by spot)/(distance moved by solvent) identifies a component and is always between 0 and 1.

```formula Key formulas
Rf = (distance moved by substance) / (distance moved by solvent front)
0 ≤ Rf ≤ 1
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** A pigment spot moves 3.0 cm while the solvent front moves 12.0 cm. Find its Rf.
> >
> > **Solution.** Rf = 3.0 / 12.0 = 0.25. Being < 1 and characteristic under those conditions, it helps identify the pigment.
