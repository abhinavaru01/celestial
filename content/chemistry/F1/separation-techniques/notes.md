# Separation Techniques

> Filtration, evaporation, distillation and chromatography — separating mixtures by physical properties.

## Separate by difference

Mixtures separate because their components differ — in particle size (filtration), boiling point (distillation) or how they travel through a medium (chromatography).

## Physical, not chemical

Separation techniques are physical: no new substance forms. Choosing the right one depends on the property that differs most.

## What you should be able to do

- Choose a separation method for a mixture
- Explain distillation and chromatography
- Relate method to particle properties
