# Matter, Particles & States

> The particle model of matter, the three states, and changes of state.

## Everything is particles

All matter is made of tiny particles in constant motion. How close and how fast they move decides whether something is solid, liquid or gas.

## Changes of state

Heating gives particles energy to move apart (melting, boiling); cooling reverses it. Mass is conserved throughout — nothing is lost, only rearranged.

## What you should be able to do

- Describe matter using the particle model
- Explain the three states and changes between them
- Distinguish pure substances from mixtures
