# Chemical Formulae & Equations

> Writing formulae and balancing equations as conservation-of-mass accounting.

## Formulae

A formula shows which atoms and how many are combined, set by their combining power (valency). It is a compact recipe for a compound.

## Balancing

A balanced equation has equal atoms of each element on both sides — because atoms are conserved. Coefficients (not subscripts) are adjusted to balance.

## What you should be able to do

- Write formulae from valencies
- Balance chemical equations
- Interpret an equation quantitatively
