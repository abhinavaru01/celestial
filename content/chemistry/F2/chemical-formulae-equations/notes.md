# Chemical Formulae & Equations

> [!intro] Writing formulae from valency and balancing equations as the visible bookkeeping of conservation of mass.

## Formulae from valency

A formula shows which atoms combine and in what ratio, set by combining power (valency). Swap-and-drop the valencies (e.g., Al³⁺ and O²⁻ → Al₂O₃).

## Balancing = conservation

A balanced equation has equal atoms of each element on both sides because atoms are conserved. Only coefficients (amounts) are adjusted — never subscripts.

```formula Key formulas
Atoms of each element: reactant side = product side
Coefficients give the mole ratio of the reaction
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Balance: Al + O₂ → Al₂O₃.
> >
> > **Solution.** 4 Al + 3 O₂ → 2 Al₂O₃. Check: Al 4=4, O 6=6. Adjust coefficients only.

## What you should be able to do

- Write formulae from valencies
- Balance chemical equations
- Interpret an equation in moles
