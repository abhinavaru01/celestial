# Atomic Structure I

> Protons, neutrons and electrons; atomic number, mass number and isotopes.

## Inside the atom

Atoms have a nucleus of protons (positive) and neutrons (neutral), surrounded by electrons (negative). The atomic number (protons) defines the element.

## Isotopes

Atoms of the same element can have different numbers of neutrons — isotopes. They share chemistry but differ in mass, which explains non-whole atomic masses.

## What you should be able to do

- Describe subatomic particles
- Use atomic number and mass number
- Explain isotopes
