# Atomic Structure I

> [!intro] Protons, neutrons and electrons; atomic number, mass number, isotopes and relative atomic mass.

## Inside the atom

A tiny dense nucleus of protons (+1) and neutrons (0) is surrounded by electrons (−1). The atomic number Z (protons) defines the element; a neutral atom has equal protons and electrons.

## Isotopes and relative atomic mass

Isotopes are atoms of one element with different neutron numbers — same chemistry, different mass. Relative atomic mass is the abundance-weighted average of isotope masses, which is why it is rarely a whole number.

```formula Key formulas
Atomic number Z = protons = electrons (neutral atom)
Mass number A = protons + neutrons
Neutrons = A − Z
Relative atomic mass = Σ(isotope mass × fractional abundance)
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** Chlorine is 75% ³⁵Cl and 25% ³⁷Cl. Find its relative atomic mass.
> >
> > **Solution.** Ar = 35(0.75) + 37(0.25) = 26.25 + 9.25 = 35.5. This weighted average is why chlorine's Ar is 35.5, not a whole number.
