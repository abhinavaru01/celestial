# The Mole Concept

> The mole and molar mass — proportional reasoning applied to counting particles (the keystone topic).

## Counting by weighing

A mole is a fixed number of particles (Avogadro's number, ~6.02×10²³). Because atoms are too small to count, we weigh them: molar mass (g/mol) links mass to number of particles.

## Ratios of particles

The mole turns particle counting into the same proportional reasoning as ratios: moles = mass ÷ molar mass. Every quantitative chemistry calculation flows from here.

## What you should be able to do

- Define the mole and Avogadro's number
- Convert between mass, moles and particles
- Use molar mass
