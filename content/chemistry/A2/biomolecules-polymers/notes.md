# Biomolecules & Polymers

> [!intro] Carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, nucleic acids and polymers — the chemistry underlying biology.

## Biomolecules

Carbohydrates, proteins, lipids and nucleic acids are built from small repeating monomers (sugars, amino acids, nucleotides) joined by condensation (losing water).

## Polymers

Polymers are long chains of monomers — natural (proteins, starch, DNA) or synthetic (polythene, PVC). Addition polymers form from alkenes; condensation polymers release a small molecule per link.

```formula Key formulas
Condensation: monomer + monomer → dimer + H₂O
Addition: n(CH₂=CH₂) → –(CH₂–CH₂)ₙ–
Proteins = polymers of amino acids (peptide bonds)
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** What type of polymerisation forms polythene from ethene, and what small molecule (if any) is lost?
> >
> > **Solution.** Addition polymerisation: the C=C bonds open and monomers add with no small molecule lost. n CH₂=CH₂ → –(CH₂–CH₂)ₙ–.
