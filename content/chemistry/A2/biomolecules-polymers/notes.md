# Biomolecules & Polymers

> Carbohydrates, proteins, lipids and polymers — chemistry that links to biology.

## Biomolecules

Carbohydrates, proteins, lipids and nucleic acids are the molecules of life. They are built from small repeating units — a chemical basis for biology.

## Polymers

Polymers are long chains of repeating monomers, natural (proteins, starch) or synthetic (plastics). Their properties come from chain length and structure.

## What you should be able to do

- Describe major biomolecules
- Explain polymerisation
- Connect structure to function
