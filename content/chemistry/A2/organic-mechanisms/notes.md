# Organic Reaction Mechanisms

> Nucleophiles, electrophiles and key reaction pathways — reasoned, not memorised.

## Electron flow

Organic reactions are electrons moving from electron-rich (nucleophile) to electron-poor (electrophile) sites. Curly arrows track this flow.

## Mechanism over memory

Understanding substitution, addition and elimination pathways lets you predict products instead of memorising thousands of reactions — the compression at the heart of organic chemistry.

## What you should be able to do

- Identify nucleophiles and electrophiles
- Follow reaction mechanisms
- Predict organic products by reasoning
