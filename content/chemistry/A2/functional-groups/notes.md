# Functional-Group Chemistry

> [!intro] Alcohols, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids and amines — characteristic reactions and interconversions.

## Groups define reactivity

Each functional group has signature reactions: alcohols oxidise, carboxylic acids neutralise bases, amines act as weak bases. Identify the group and you predict the chemistry.

## Oxidation ladder

Primary alcohol → aldehyde → carboxylic acid on oxidation; secondary alcohol → ketone (no further). These routes build target molecules.

```formula Key formulas
–OH alcohol, –CHO aldehyde, C=O ketone, –COOH acid, –NH₂ amine
Primary alcohol —[O]→ aldehyde —[O]→ carboxylic acid
Secondary alcohol —[O]→ ketone (stops)
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** What is formed when ethanol (a primary alcohol) is fully oxidised?
> >
> > **Solution.** Ethanol → ethanal (aldehyde) → ethanoic acid. Full oxidation gives the carboxylic acid, ethanoic (acetic) acid.

## What you should be able to do

- Recognise major functional groups
- Predict characteristic reactions
- Plan functional-group interconversions
