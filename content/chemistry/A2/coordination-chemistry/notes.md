# d-Block & Coordination Chemistry

> Transition metals, complex ions, ligands and colour.

## Transition metals

d-block elements show variable oxidation states, form coloured compounds and act as catalysts — all traceable to their partly filled d-orbitals.

## Complexes

A central metal ion bonds to surrounding ligands, forming a coordination complex. Ligand arrangement splits d-orbital energies, producing characteristic colours.

## What you should be able to do

- Describe transition-metal properties
- Explain coordination complexes
- Relate d-electrons to colour
