# Analytical & Practical Chemistry Portfolio

> Qualitative and quantitative analysis, and error treatment — the capstone lab strand.

## Identifying and measuring

Qualitative analysis identifies what is present (flame tests, precipitates); quantitative analysis measures how much (titration, gravimetry).

## Reliable results

Good practical chemistry controls variables, repeats measurements and reports uncertainty — reusing the statistics and error skills from maths and physics.

## What you should be able to do

- Perform qualitative tests
- Carry out quantitative analysis
- Treat experimental error
