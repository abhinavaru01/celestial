# Analytical & Practical Chemistry Portfolio

> [!intro] Qualitative and quantitative analysis (including titration calculations) and treatment of experimental error — the capstone lab strand.

## Identifying and measuring

Qualitative analysis identifies what is present (flame tests, precipitates, gas tests); quantitative analysis measures how much (titration, gravimetry).

## Titration calculations

From a titration, moles of one reactant give moles of the other via the equation, then concentration. Repeat for concordant results and report uncertainty.

```formula Key formulas
n = C × V (V in litres)
At the end point: n(acid)/n(base) = mole ratio from the equation
Concordant titres agree within ~0.10 cm³
```

> [!example] **Worked example**
> **Problem.** 25.0 cm³ of NaOH is neutralised by 20.0 cm³ of 0.100 M HCl (1:1). Find the NaOH concentration.
> >
> > **Solution.** n(HCl) = 0.100 × 0.0200 = 0.00200 mol. 1:1 → n(NaOH) = 0.00200 mol. C = n/V = 0.00200/0.0250 = 0.080 M.
